Leaf Removal From the Lawn and Landscape Beds

A lawn covered with loose leaves is a different cleanup from wet leaves packed into shrub beds or along a fence. Include both areas in your request, along with any light yard debris you want removed. Leaf collection and removal can be quoted as a one-time job; photos help show the amount of material and access for the cleanup.

Fall and Spring Yard Cleanup Pricing

Leaf and seasonal cleanups start at $150. Final pricing depends on property size, the volume and condition of material, and the work needed in beds as well as on the lawn. If leaves are still falling, discuss whether you want one cleanup or help at more than one point in the season. Heavy or hazardous storm work needs a separate discussion of the scope.

Questions About Leaf & Seasonal Cleanups

Are leaf cleanups available as a one-time service?

Yes. One-time leaf removal and seasonal property cleanups are available for homes and commercial properties. Request a free estimate and include the address and areas you want cleared.

Does the starting price apply to every yard?

No. The $150 figure is a starting price. Leaf volume, property size, access, and the amount of bed and lawn cleanup determine the final estimate before work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, every estimate is free. Fill out the estimate form on the homepage, or call or text (417) 849-7131 and we'll get you a price for your property.

Are you insured?

Yes, ET's Lawn Care & More is fully insured. If you need proof of insurance for an HOA or a commercial property manager, just ask and we'll send it over.

How long have you been in business?

Five years. Eli started ET's Lawn Care & More to do dependable, high-quality work around Springfield, and it's grown on repeat customers and referrals since.

What does mowing cost?

Mowing starts at a $50 minimum. Larger properties run $95 to $135 per acre depending on terrain, obstacles, and how tall the grass has gotten. Send in the estimate form and you'll get an exact price for your property, not a guess.

What does a leaf cleanup cost?

Leaf and seasonal cleanups start at a $150 minimum. Where it lands above that depends on the size of the property and how heavy the leaf cover is.

How is landscaping or aeration priced?

Per project, because no two properties are alike. Landscaping, aeration and overseeding, mulch and rock, hardscaping, and pressure washing all get a free on-site estimate rather than a one-size-fits-all number.

What areas do you service?

We're based in Springfield, MO and cover the surrounding towns — Ozark, Nixa, Republic, Branson, Bolivar, and dozens more. For bigger jobs we'll travel over 75 miles, so if you're farther out than you'd expect, still reach out and ask.

Do you work with businesses, or just homes?

Both. Residential properties and commercial accounts alike — office buildings, lots, and hedge rows included. Commercial work can be scheduled around your business hours.

Do you offer one-time services, or only recurring?

Both. Plenty of customers start with a single mow or a one-time cleanup, and many move to a regular schedule once they see the work. There's no pressure to commit upfront.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. You can schedule weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or one-time service, and change or cancel it as your needs change.

What if it rains on my scheduled mowing day?

We'll move you to the next available day rather than mow a soaked lawn, which tears up the turf and leaves ruts. You don't need to do anything — we'll handle the rescheduling and keep you posted.

How quickly can you get to a new customer?

It depends on the season and how the route is running, but we move fast on new customers. Reach out and you'll get a straight answer on timing for your area.

What else do you do besides mowing?

Landscaping and bed cleanups, leaf and seasonal cleanups, aeration and overseeding, fertilization and weed control, mulch and rock installation, shrub trimming, pavers and small hardscaping, pressure washing, and snow removal. If it's an outdoor project on your property, ask — that's what the "& More" is for.

Fall Lawn Cleanup Checklist for Missouri Homeowners

By Eli, owner of ET’s Lawn Care & More

A thick layer of wet leaves left on the lawn all winter blocks sunlight and traps moisture against the grass, which can lead to matted, damaged patches come spring. Regular leaf removal through fall is one of the highest-value things you can do for your lawn's health.

Beyond leaves, fall cleanup is a good time to clear out summer's dead annuals, cut back perennials, and pull any lingering debris out of garden beds before mulching for winter.

Fall is also the ideal window for cool-season lawns to recover from summer stress — pairing a cleanup with fall fertilization and aeration gives grass the best shot at a strong root system before winter dormancy.

We offer fall cleanup as a one-time service or bundled with a seasonal treatment plan, whichever fits your yard.

How Much Does Lawn Mowing Cost in Springfield, MO?

By Eli, owner of ET’s Lawn Care & More

ET's Lawn Care & More has a $50 minimum for mowing service around Springfield, Missouri. Larger mowing jobs typically run $95–$135 per acre, depending on the property. Those figures help you plan, but the final price comes from a free estimate for the actual lawn and the work involved.

The minimum and the acreage range describe different situations. A small yard does not automatically cost a fraction of the per-acre rate: travel, unloading, mowing, trimming, and cleanup still take time. For a larger property, the open area may be quick to cut while fences, trees, and outbuildings add detail work. Tell Eli which parts of the parcel you want maintained so the estimate is based on the mowable area.

Grass height and condition also matter. A lawn that is already maintained can involve different work from a one-time cut after weeks of growth. Include a recent photo or describe the height, wet spots, slopes, and obstacles when asking for a quote. That helps define the first visit as well as the recurring service you may want afterward.

ET's mowing service includes cutting at an appropriate height, trimming around trees, fences, beds, and structures, and clearing clippings from hard surfaces. Leaf removal, shrub trimming, landscape bed cleanup, and new mulch or rock are separate types of work that can be discussed in the same estimate request. Leaf cleanups start at $150, while landscaping is priced for the project.

Frequency should fit the lawn's growth. Weekly, bi-weekly, and one-time mowing options are available, with the schedule discussed for your property and current conditions. A longer interval does not necessarily mean the same amount of work at each visit, particularly when grass has grown tall. Ask Eli to explain the proposed scope and schedule before deciding.

To request a useful estimate, send the property address, approximate lawn size, preferred frequency, and access details. Mention gates, pets, parked vehicles, or areas that need extra trimming. If you manage a commercial property, include the exact areas you want maintained and any access arrangements the crew needs to follow.

Springfield, Ozark, Nixa, Republic, Willard, and Fair Grove are priority service areas. For other addresses, Eli will confirm route availability before scheduling. Estimates are free, and one-time or recurring service is available without a contract. Use the website estimate form or call or text (417) 849-7131 to get a price for your lawn.
